GTAHUB — GTA 6 Interactive Map & Wiki

GTAHUB is a fan-made GTA 6 hub. The interactive map of the state of Leonida comes with a drive mode, collectible cards, comparisons with earlier GTA maps and a story board. Explore the site and earn Respect. Plus a wiki with 2100 pages in 11 languages and daily news.

Explore Vice City and the rest of Leonida, read about Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, and check vehicles, weapons, gangs and a breakdown of every trailer.

GTA 6 releases on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. GTAHUB is a fan project, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
